Traveling & Booking FAQs

1 Booking & Payments

How do I book a trip or tour package with Travel World? +

Choose your preferred destination or package, select your travel dates and group size, and complete your reservation online or through our travel specialists on the About & Contact page. You will receive an instant confirmation email with your itinerary summary.

Do I have to pay the full amount upfront, or can I pay a deposit? +

You can secure most trips booked more than 45 days in advance with a 20% deposit. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to departure, and we accept major credit cards, debit cards, and bank transfers.

Are international flights included in tour package prices? +

Most Travel World packages cover accommodations, local transportation, guided activities, and select meals so travelers can book flights from any city. Optional flight add-ons are available upon request when finalizing your booking.

2 Changes & Cancellations

What is your cancellation and refund policy? +

Cancellations made at least 30 days before departure qualify for a full refund minus any non-refundable partner fees. Cancellations between 15 and 29 days prior receive a 50% refund or a 100% travel credit toward a future trip.

Can I change my travel dates or customize my itinerary after booking? +

Yes! Date changes and itinerary adjustments are free of charge up to 30 days before your trip starts, subject to hotel and tour availability. Simply reach out to our support team with your booking reference number.

3 Travel Prep, Passports & Insurance

What passport and visa requirements should I check before traveling? +

Your passport should generally be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned return date. Because entry and visa rules vary by nationality and destination, we send a destination checklist after booking and recommend verifying requirements with your local embassy.

Is travel insurance required for Travel World trips? +

Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip delays, and cancellations is strongly recommended for all travelers and required for adventure trekking or safari expeditions.

4 During Your Trip

Are airport transfers and local guides included? +

Yes, scheduled arrival and departure airport transfers are included for every multi-day package, along with certified English-speaking local guides for all listed excursions.

How can I get help if an issue comes up while I am traveling? +

Every traveler receives access to our 24/7 emergency assistance hotline and dedicated trip coordinator contact details in their pre-departure welcome packet, so help is always a quick call or message away.
